The Show

A cinematic journey in sound — scaled to the room, from one musician to a full band.

Epic Scores Live is an evening of the greatest music ever written for screens: Interstellar, Wonder Woman, The Legend of Zelda, and the themes audiences have carried since childhood.

Christian Grosselfinger performs them on cello, electric guitar and keyboard, bringing the whole orchestra to the stage — strings, rhythm, counter-melody — until a single musician sounds like a full score. The lineup is flexible: solo by default, and where the venue allows, it grows — a drummer joins first, then further musicians as conditions permit. Sebastian Schneider and Aileen Schneider are his first call, with local players welcome when they can't make it.

It is a night of nostalgia, power and emotion, where sound and storytelling collide. Gamers recognise the first bar. Cinema audiences recognise the second. Everyone recognises the feeling.

Best suited to cinemas, theatres, cultural centres, gaming conventions and festivals — anywhere a room can go dark and be taken somewhere.

For Venues & Promoters

Epic Scores Live sounds enormous and travels light. Everything runs internally through the artist’s own system — if you have a PA and a mixer, there is almost nothing for you to prepare. The lineup is flexible, from solo one-man orchestra to a full band — get in touch to discuss what fits your stage.

On stage
1–4 musicians (flexible)
Duration
60–90 min, straight through or with interval
Minimum stage
3 × 2 m
Set-up
1 hour with house PA & lights
Venue provides
2 × XLR (stereo) · power on stage · one chair without arms
Projection
Screen + HDMI projector strongly recommended

DI boxes, microphone and monitoring are carried by the artist; a 2-channel desk is sufficient. A full DMX lighting plot is available on request.
